# Quick Reference — TX/RX Pins & Cable Selection

## Pin Assignments by Device

| Device  | Transmits (TX) | Receives (RX) |
|---------|----------------|---------------|
| PC      | 1, 2           | 3, 6          |
| Server  | 1, 2           | 3, 6          |
| Router  | 1, 2           | 3, 6          |
| Switch  | 3, 6           | 1, 2          |
| Hub     | 3, 6           | 1, 2          |

---

## The Two Groups

**Group A** (TX on 1 & 2): PC, Server, Router  
**Group B** (TX on 3 & 6): Switch, Hub

- **A ↔ B** = Straight-through  
- **A ↔ A** = Crossover  
- **B ↔ B** = Crossover  

---

## Fiber Distance Guide

| Distance       | Fiber Type     |
|----------------|----------------|
| 0 – 100m       | UTP Copper     |
| 100m – 550m    | Multimode Fiber|
| 550m+          | Single-Mode Fiber |

---

## One-Line Summary

> Connect same → crossover. Connect different → straight-through. Too far for copper → fiber. Too far for multimode → single-mode.
